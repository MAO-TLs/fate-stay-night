- Correct a weapon reference identified by the Japanese scene’s Archer cut-in, plus four smaller source-backed corrections in that scene.
- Normalize 30 mid-sentence verb/adjective capitalizations, including “reinforced” and “projected.” Named techniques and sentence-initial capitals are preserved.

- Repair eight source-backed text/voice boundaries where dialogue prefixes or narration had been assigned to the wrong side of a voice cue.
- Repair malformed original/all-ages sentence joins and bring the two previously browser-only corrections into the game patch.
- Synchronize 37 browser passages, their edition counterparts, and search data. Existing passage links remain valid.

The patch changes 57 pages across 40 script entries. All 895 archive entries, 734 script files, 27,918 native page labels, non-script assets, and existing voice/action command lines are preserved. Text moves around the existing cues according to the Japanese source; this does not replace voice recordings.
